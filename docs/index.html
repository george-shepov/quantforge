Hand-checkable fixtures

Simple enough for a whiteboard.

Start with examples where the expected answer is obvious. Then move to depth, partial fills, and full replay. Complexity should be earned, not hidden.

Accepted example

Buy $100 · Sell $101 · 2 units · 1 bp per leg

gross = (101 − 100) ÷ 100 × 10,000 = 100 bps
expected = 100 − 2 = 98 bps
scanner estimate = 100 × 2 × .0098 = $1.9600
exact fee cash P&L = 202 − 200 − .0200 − .0202 = $1.9598

The $0.0002 difference is disclosed: the scanner uses a compact basis-point estimate; replay charges each leg using its actual simulated notional.

Rejected example

Buy $100 · Sell $100.05 · 0.4 units · 2 bps per leg

gross = (100.05 − 100) ÷ 100 × 10,000 = 5 bps
expected = 5 − 4 = 1 bp
minimum required = 5 bps
decision = REJECTED

Rejected rows remain evidence. They show the arithmetic and the exact threshold reason rather than disappearing from the research record.

Claim boundary

What this proof means.

QuantForge is a research laboratory. A credible laboratory states both what an experiment establishes and what remains unresolved.

✓ The certificate can establish

  • Formula implementation for the included known-answer fixtures
  • Depth-weighted price and partial-fill arithmetic
  • Per-fill fee accounting and portfolio identities
  • Deterministic replay for a fixed event stream
  • Seed reproducibility for Monte Carlo resampling

△ It does not establish

  • Future strategy profitability
  • That every displayed opportunity was executable live
  • Exact exchange queue position, latency, or matching behavior
  • That synthetic fallback data equals a live market
  • Correctness for every possible input and every strategy
